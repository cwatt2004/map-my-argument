How does scoring work?

Required premises are multiplied as independent necessary propositions. Supporting and opposing premises make illustrative adjustments using their confidence and selected strength (Irrelevant 0, Weak 0.1, Moderate 0.25, Strong 0.5, Decisive 0.8). The result is a discussion aid, not a statistically established probability. Premises can be correlated, so scores can be misleading. Group scores average individual scores.
